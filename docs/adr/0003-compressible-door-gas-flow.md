# Compressible Door flow with inventory-dependent gas energy

[ADR-0004](0004-cell-atmosphere-and-horizontal-momentum.md) governs current spatial Editor playback. This ADR governs the standalone compartment runtime; the two gas/heat runtimes are not run together.

**Status: Accepted.** Explicit Door pathways use compressible ideal-gas orifice flow for both inter-Sector openings and exterior venting, replacing the old diffusion-only bulk law. Raising a diffusion coefficient would not reproduce choking or conserve the energy of a rapidly emptying compartment. The no-inferred-pathways and dependency boundaries of ADR-0001/0002 remain unchanged.

Bulk gas flows from higher to lower absolute pressure. With upstream mixture gamma = cp/cv, it chokes at downstream/upstream ratio `(2/(gamma+1))^(gamma/(gamma-1))`; otherwise the subsonic isentropic orifice relation applies. A Door's clear opening is 0.9 m × 2 m (1.8 m²), not its full Cell-edge Wall surface. A provisional discharge coefficient of 0.7 multiplies open area (allowed range `(0,1]`); it is a per-pathway parameter, not a second area or a calibrated Door specification. Existing Installations keep their authored geometry and default coefficient; Station configuration schema 140 writes the coefficient, and earlier documents load with 0.7. There is no legacy bulk-flow mode.

Every compartment participating in a pathway, even a closed one, uses gas-only internal energy `sum(m_i cv_i) T_K` and inventory-dependent capacity. Advected energy is upstream enthalpy `sum(dm_i cp_i) T_K`, booked once with opposite signs at the endpoints or as explicit exterior loss. Constant near-room-temperature species cp values are engineering approximations: O2 0.918, N2 1.040, CO2 0.844 and water vapour 1.860 kJ/(kg K); `cv_i = cp_i - R_i`. Authored fixed capacity and bulk advected-specific-heat fields remain on the wire but do not create hidden wall heat mass on these compartments. Standalone atmosphere nodes without pathways retain their existing explicitly authored fixed-capacity contract. Hull/partition heat exchange is still separate. Thus the volumetric cv estimate in ADR-0001 remains a starting authoring estimate, not a fixed runtime gas capacity for Door-connected compartments.

Non-pathway gas additions/removals have a separately exposed sensible-internal-energy account at the compartment temperature. The existing metabolism, leakage, machines, airlock schedules and condensate ledgers therefore remain auditable rather than becoming unreported changes in gas capacity. Existing latent-heat/condensation treatment is retained; ice, real-gas effects, temperature-dependent cp, detailed jets and low-pressure corrections to Hull U-values are outside this approximation.

Residual composition mixing is molar counterdiffusion, not a second bulk pressure-equalisation solver. It moves each species with its own upstream enthalpy and can operate at equal total pressure; no mixing reservoir exists outside. Conservative common substeps (at most 2 ms, limited by 1% donor mass per edge) and pressure-crossing bisection prevent donor overdraft and pair-equilibrium overshoot. Substeps have a finite work budget; pressures below 1e-6 Pa and relative pressure differences below 1e-9 are below the resolved bulk-flow range. The heat solver retains its 1e-7 kJ energy budget on trace-gas nodes rather than demanding nanokelvin temperature agreement there, and stiff multi-contact meshes use a simultaneous conservative implicit solve with second-order extrapolation. This avoids divergent pair-splitting work as gas capacity vanishes, without introducing structural heat mass. This is a well-mixed compartment model, not CFD or an acoustic-wave solver.

Pathway readouts distinguish current sealed/no-bulk-flow/subsonic/choked regime and downstream/upstream pressure ratio from last-step-average signed bulk/species mass flow and enthalpy flow. Regression coverage includes analytic dry choked blowdown, sonic-transition continuity, equal-pressure mixing, network conservation, timestep refinement, runtime energy audits and legacy loading defaults.
