# Passive aerodynamic loads and optional diagnostics

**Status: Accepted.** Extend ADR-0004's gas-only scope with native, one-way aerodynamic-load sampling and a reference-object overlay. Wind velocity alone is not a measure of the push on an object: decompression can leave similar velocities while density and load collapse. We expose force estimates rather than inventing attraction toward a breach.

The reference object is stationary with projected area 1 m², displaced volume 1 m³ and drag coefficient one. For an actual object's supplied area, volume, Cd and velocity, the estimate is `F_drag = 0.5 rho Cd A |u-v| (u-v)` plus `F_pressure = -V grad(p)`. These are engineering estimates for a small passive object in the undisturbed Cell field, not resolved surface stresses. Shape/orientation enter through the caller's area/Cd. Lift, torque, added mass, transient history, Mach/rarefied corrections, obstruction, collisions, friction and rigid-body motion are not implemented.

The Cell-centred pressure gradient is a finite-volume reconstruction. Open faces use mean adjacent pressure, with vacuum outside; closed portions use owner pressure. Thus a face contributes `n (p_b-p_a) A_effective / (2 V_cell)` to each endpoint, with the appropriate Cell volume. This matches the existing aperture/Cd and storage-height approximation, does not sample pressure across closed Walls or between Levels, and updates immediately when a Door changes. It is deliberately a coarse volume-average, not a calibrated sub-Cell jet/nozzle or object-scale gradient.

Sampling never alters gas, momentum, energy, authored Installations or time. Applying these forces to future moving bodies would require a separate motion/contact model; conservative two-way coupling would additionally require transactional opposite gas bookings. We do not claim those exist merely because a force is available.

Independent runtime controls, both initially enabled, select wind diagnostics and object-force calculations. Wind-off suppresses optional speed/vector observations; the conservative gas solver still requires velocity and kinetic energy. Force-on uses that solver velocity even with wind diagnostics off. Force-off skips gradient/load updates and invalidates previous force observations/queries. Controls survive preview resets/document replacement, do not advance time or add history, and are not Installation properties. Unresolved or unrepresentable loads are unavailable, never measured zero.

Airflow-load arrows replace speed-only arrows. Direction is the reference object's resultant force; length is proportional to `|F| / (|F| + scale)` with an adjustable half-length scale in newtons. There is no fixed minimum length or hard speed saturation; subpixel arrows are omitted. Raw wind, density, dynamic pressure and separate drag/pressure terms remain inspectable when their calculations are enabled. This makes evacuating rooms lose visible load before the gas becomes numerically unresolved.
